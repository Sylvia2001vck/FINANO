Part 1 Proposal Overview 
--- Slide 4 ---

1.1 What is FINANO?
FINANO is an AI multi-agent engine that upgrades how a bank profiles each client's behavior and recommends suitable funds.
The gap it fills: Banks today match clients to products by their money;  FINANO adds the missing layer — matching by behavior.
* FINANO augments, not replaces, a bank's existing KYC flow.

Three engines, one closed loop that knows each client better over time.
