1.1 What is FINANO?
---
FINANO is an AI multi-agent engine that upgrades how a bank profiles each client's behavior and recommends suitable funds.
---
The gap it fills:
Banks today match clients to products by their money; 
FINANO adds the missing layer — matching by behavior.
---
* FINANO augments, not replaces, a bank's existing KYC flow. 
---
【繁體 · HK】FINANO 係生成式 AI 多智能體引擎，補足「按投資行為配對基金」呢一層；強化而唔取代銀行 KYC。【粵語口播】銀行今日多數按資產同風險等級配產品；FINANO 多加一層——睇你幾時追升、幾時恐慌性賣出，先至叫真正適當。
